## Quick-start (put this on page 1)

These 50 prompts are built to work with ChatGPT, Gemini, or Claude. Anything in
`[BRACKETS]` is a fill-in — replace it with your details before you send. For best
results, paste a prompt, swap the variables, and then add one line of context
("My brand voice is warm and concise"). Start with the **Listings** and **Social
Media** sections for instant daily wins, then layer in lead-gen and email
automation as you scale. Copy, customize, send.

## 1. Listing Descriptions & Property Marketing

**1. MLS Listing Description**
> Write a compelling MLS listing description for a `[BEDROOMS]`-bed,
> `[BATHROOMS]`-bath `[PROPERTY TYPE]` at `[NEIGHBORHOOD/CITY]`, listed at
> `[PRICE]`. Lead with the single best feature, weave in these highlights:
> `[TOP 3–5 FEATURES]`, paint the `[NEIGHBORHOOD]` lifestyle, and close with a
> clear call to action. Tone: `[LUXURY / FAMILY-FRIENDLY / STARTER HOME]`. Keep
> it under 1,500 characters and avoid fair-housing-sensitive language.
*Use this when you need a polished listing live in minutes.*

**2. Luxury Listing Description**
> Write an elevated, evocative listing description for a luxury
> `[PROPERTY TYPE]` in `[AREA]` priced at `[PRICE]`. Emphasize craftsmanship,
> exclusivity, and lifestyle over a feature checklist. Highlight
> `[SIGNATURE FEATURES]` and the `[VIEW / LOCATION / AMENITY]`. Sophisticated,
> aspirational tone; no clichés like "must see."
*Use this when listing high-end properties where tone signals price.*

**3. Just Listed Announcement**
> Write a short "Just Listed" announcement for `[ADDRESS/AREA]`, a `[PRICE]`
> `[PROPERTY TYPE]`. Give me three versions: one for email, one for a text blast
> to my database, and one for a Facebook post. Each should create urgency and
> drive replies.
*Use this when a new listing goes live and you want every channel covered.*

**4. Feature Sheet Bullets**
> Turn these raw property notes into a clean, scannable feature sheet with short
> punchy bullets grouped by Interior, Exterior, and Neighborhood: `[PASTE NOTES]`.
*Use this when you need a print-ready highlight sheet for showings.*

**5. Open House Description**
> Write an inviting open house promo for `[ADDRESS]` on `[DATE/TIME]`. Include a
> hook, 3 reasons to attend, and a CTA. Give me a 280-character social version and
> a longer email version.
*Use this when driving foot traffic to a weekend open house.*

**6. Price Reduction Announcement**
> Write a positive, momentum-building "Price Improvement" announcement for
> `[ADDRESS]`, now `[NEW PRICE]` (was `[OLD PRICE]`). Frame it as opportunity, not
> desperation. Email + social versions.
*Use this when a price drop needs to spark fresh interest, not red flags.*

**7. Coming Soon Teaser**
> Write a "Coming Soon" teaser for a `[PROPERTY TYPE]` in `[AREA]` hitting the
> market `[DATE]`. Build anticipation, hint at the best feature, and invite people
> to DM for early access.
*Use this when building a buyer waitlist before you go live.*

**8. Rental Listing Description**
> Write a rental listing for a `[BEDROOMS]`-bed `[PROPERTY TYPE]` at `[AREA]`,
> `[RENT]`/month. Cover key amenities `[LIST]`, lease terms `[TERMS]`, and pet/
> parking policy. Friendly, clear, and skimmable.
*Use this when listing a rental and want qualified inquiries fast.*

**9. 60-Second Property Video Script**
> Write a 60-second walkthrough video script for `[ADDRESS]`. Hook in the first 3
> seconds, highlight `[3 FEATURES]` room by room, and end with a CTA to book a
> showing. Include on-screen text suggestions.
*Use this when filming a reel or listing video.*

**10. Neighborhood & Community Description**
> Write a warm, factual neighborhood overview for `[NEIGHBORHOOD, CITY]` covering
> lifestyle, commute, parks, dining, and what makes it special — for buyers
> relocating from out of area. Avoid steering or fair-housing-sensitive claims.
*Use this when relocating buyers need to picture the area.*

## 2. Social Media & Content

**11. Just Listed Instagram Caption**
> Write 3 scroll-stopping Instagram captions for a Just Listed `[PROPERTY TYPE]`
> in `[AREA]` at `[PRICE]`. Vary the angle (feature, lifestyle, urgency). Add

*Use this when posting a new listing to Instagram.*

**12. Just Sold Caption**
> Write a celebratory "Just Sold" caption for `[AREA]`. Subtly position my results
> (e.g., `[DAYS ON MARKET]`, `[% OF ASKING]`) without bragging, and invite sellers
> who are thinking of listing to reach out.
*Use this when a deal closes and you want it to generate seller leads.*

**13. Open House Story Series**
> Write a 5-frame Instagram Story sequence promoting my open house at `[ADDRESS]`
> on `[DATE/TIME]`. Frame-by-frame text plus a poll or question sticker idea for

*Use this when driving open-house attendance via Stories.*

**14. Monthly Market Update Post**
> Turn these stats into an easy-to-understand market update post for `[AREA]`:
> `[MEDIAN PRICE, DOM, INVENTORY, % CHANGE]`. Explain what it means for buyers and

*Use this when posting your monthly market authority content.*

**15. Buyer Tips Carousel**
> Write a 5-slide Instagram carousel: "`[NUMBER]` things first-time buyers in
> `[AREA]` need to know." One tip per slide, short and punchy, with a caption and
> CTA.
*Use this when creating educational content that attracts buyers.*

**16. Seller Tips Carousel**
> Write a 5-slide carousel: "How to sell your `[AREA]` home for more in
> `[YEAR/SEASON]`." Actionable, specific tips per slide, with caption and CTA.

**17. Meet Your Agent Post**
> Write a warm "meet your agent" intro post for me: `[NAME]`, `[YEARS]` years in
> `[MARKET]`, specializing in `[NICHE]`. Make it personable, build trust, and end
> with how to start working with me.
*Use this when introducing yourself to new followers.*

**18. Testimonial Repost Caption**
> Turn this client review into an engaging social caption that highlights the
> result and invites similar clients to reach out: `[PASTE REVIEW]`.
*Use this when reposting a client testimonial.*

**19. 10 Reel Hooks**
> Give me 10 scroll-stopping first-line hooks for real estate Reels aimed at
> `[BUYERS / SELLERS / INVESTORS]` in `[AREA]`. Make them curiosity-driven and
> specific.
*Use this when you need video ideas that actually get watched.*

**20. Local Business Spotlight**
> Write a friendly post spotlighting `[LOCAL BUSINESS]` in `[NEIGHBORHOOD]`.
> Position me as the connected local expert, tag-friendly, with a soft CTA.
*Use this when building local goodwill and community reach.*

## 3. Lead Generation & Prospecting

**21. Expired Listing Letter**
> Write a tactful, confident letter to the owner of an expired listing at
> `[AREA]`. Acknowledge their frustration, hint at why it may not have sold, and
> offer a fresh strategy. Warm, not salesy. Include a clear next step.
*Use this when farming expired listings.*

**22. FSBO Outreach Script**
> Write a phone/DM script to contact a For-Sale-By-Owner in `[AREA]`. Lead with
> value (not a pitch), offer help, and gently surface the benefits of
> representation. Include 2 objection responses.
*Use this when approaching FSBO sellers.*

**23. Circle Prospecting Script**
> Write a "just sold/just listed in your neighborhood" circle-prospecting script
> for calling homeowners near `[ADDRESS]`. Short, friendly, and designed to start
> a conversation about their home's value.
*Use this when farming a neighborhood around a sale.*

**24. Open House Follow-Up Text**
> Write 3 follow-up text messages to send the day after my open house to a visitor
> named `[NAME]` who saw `[ADDRESS]`: one for a hot buyer, one for a maybe, one for
> someone who's also a potential seller.
*Use this when following up open-house sign-ins.*

**25. Renter-to-Buyer Script**
> Write a script to start a conversation with a renter in `[AREA]` about whether
> buying could cost less than renting. Include a simple rent-vs-buy framing and a
> low-pressure CTA.
*Use this when converting renters into buyer leads.*

**26. Sphere of Influence Check-In**
> Write a casual, non-salesy check-in message to past clients and friends in my
> sphere. Goal: stay top of mind and gently invite referrals. Give me text and
> email versions.
*Use this when nurturing your database monthly.*

**27. Home-aversary Message**
> Write a warm "happy home-aversary" message for a client who bought
> `[TIMEFRAME]` ago. Celebrate them, offer a free updated home valuation, and keep
> it personal.
*Use this when re-engaging past buyers on their purchase anniversary.*

**28. Buyer Guide Lead Magnet**
> Outline a free "`[AREA]` First-Time Buyer Guide" lead magnet and write the
> landing-page copy (headline, 3 bullets of what's inside, email opt-in CTA) to
> capture buyer leads.
*Use this when building a lead-capture funnel.*

**29. Seller Lead Ad Copy**
> Write 3 Facebook/Google ad variations targeting homeowners in `[AREA]` who may
> want to sell. Hook + value + CTA ("What's your home worth?"). Keep each under
> `[CHAR LIMIT]`.
*Use this when running paid seller-lead campaigns.*

**30. Door-Knocking Script**
> Write a brief, confident door-knocking script for `[NEIGHBORHOOD]` introducing
> myself, offering a recent-sales update, and asking one engaging question.
> Include a graceful exit line.
*Use this when door-knocking a farm area.*

## 4. Client Communication & Emails

**31. New Buyer Welcome Email**
> Write a welcome email for a new buyer lead `[NAME]`. Set expectations, outline
> the next 3 steps (pre-approval, search, showings), and convey calm expertise.
> End with a scheduling CTA.
*Use this when a new buyer enters your pipeline.*

**32. New Seller Welcome Email**
> Write a welcome email for a new seller lead `[NAME]` for their `[AREA]` home.
> Outline your process, what you'll handle, and the first step (a listing
> consultation). Reassuring and professional.
*Use this when onboarding a new seller.*

**33. Listing Presentation Follow-Up**
> Write a follow-up email after a listing appointment with `[NAME]`. Recap the
> plan, reinforce 2 reasons to choose me, address the likely hesitation
> `[OBJECTION]`, and ask for the go-ahead.
*Use this when closing a seller after the pitch.*

**34. Offer Accepted — Next Steps**
> Write a congratulations email to `[NAME]` whose offer was accepted on
> `[ADDRESS]`. List the next milestones (inspection, appraisal, financing,
> closing) with dates `[FILL]` and reassure them you'll guide each step.
*Use this when an offer gets accepted.*

**35. Offer Not Accepted / Counter**
> Write an encouraging email to buyer `[NAME]` explaining their offer wasn't
> accepted (or got a counter of `[TERMS]`). Reframe positively, lay out options,
> and recommend a next move.
*Use this when managing buyer disappointment.*

**36. Weekly Seller Update**
> Write a weekly update email to seller `[NAME]` for `[ADDRESS]`: showings this
> week `[#]`, feedback themes `[NOTES]`, market activity, and your recommendation.
> Transparent and proactive.
*Use this when keeping listing clients informed.*

**37. Inspection Issues Explainer**
> Write an email to buyer `[NAME]` summarizing inspection findings `[LIST]` in
> plain language, separating "must address" from "minor," and recommending a
> negotiation approach.
*Use this when walking a client through inspection results.*

**38. Closing Day Instructions**
> Write a clear closing-day email for `[NAME]`: what to bring, funds/wire details
> reminder (with fraud-warning note), timing, and what to expect. Calm and
> organized.
*Use this when prepping a client for closing.*

**39. Post-Closing Thank You + Review Request**
> Write a heartfelt post-closing thank-you to `[NAME]` and naturally ask for a
> review, with a direct link placeholder `[REVIEW LINK]` and a referral mention.
*Use this when wrapping a deal and generating reviews/referrals.*

**40. Re-Engage a Cold Lead**
> Write a low-pressure re-engagement email to a lead `[NAME]` who went quiet
> `[TIMEFRAME]` ago. Provide a piece of value (market update or new listing) and a
> soft, easy CTA to reply.
*Use this when reviving dormant leads.*

**42. Lowball Offer Response**

**43. CMA Narrative**
> Turn these comps into a persuasive pricing narrative for `[ADDRESS]`:
> `[PASTE COMPS]`. Explain the recommended list price `[PRICE]` and why, in terms

*Use this when presenting a comparative market analysis.*

**44. Objection: "Your Commission Is Too High"**
> Write a confident, respectful response to the objection "your commission is too

*Use this when defending your fee.*

**45. Objection: "We Want to Wait for the Market"**
> Write a balanced response to sellers who want to wait for a better market.
> Acknowledge the concern, share a data-informed perspective for `[AREA]`, and
> outline the risks/benefits of waiting vs. acting.
*Use this when sellers are hesitating on timing.*

**46. Referral Request Script**
> Write a natural script (text + email) to ask happy past clients for referrals

*Use this when systematically generating referrals.*

**47. Agent-to-Agent Referral Email**
> Write a professional email to an out-of-area agent referring my client `[NAME]`

*Use this when sending or building referral partnerships.*

**48. 90-Day Prospecting Plan**
> Build me a 90-day prospecting plan to hit `[GOAL: e.g., 3 listings]`. Break it

> targets and a simple tracking method.
*Use this when you need structure and accountability.*

**49. Monthly Newsletter**
> Write my monthly email newsletter for `[AREA]`: a short market snapshot, one
> useful tip, a featured listing `[ADDRESS]`, and a personal note. Friendly, brief,
> and skimmable.
*Use this when staying top-of-mind with your database.*

**50. Review Response Templates**
> Write professional responses to client reviews: 2 for glowing 5-star reviews
> (gracious, specific) and 2 for a critical review `[PASTE REVIEW]` (empathetic,

*Use this when managing your online reputation.*
