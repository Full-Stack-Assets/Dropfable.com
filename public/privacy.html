Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

Dropfable is operated by Full Stack Assets. This policy describes what we collect when you use dropfable.com.

Data we process

  • Google sign-in identity if you authenticate with Firebase.
  • Archived products stored in your browser and, when signed in, in Firestore.
  • Niche prompts and generated content sent to the API and model providers (Google Gemini and, if configured, OpenAI).
  • Optional billing email and usage associated with an API key.
  • Analytics events when VITE_GA_MEASUREMENT_ID is configured (tool start/complete/error, listing and sale notes).

Cookies and consent

Analytics loads only when a measurement ID is set. Advertising features are not enabled. Do not treat this page as a substitute for a consent banner if you later enable ads.

Retention

You can delete archive items in the product. Signed-in archives live in your Firebase user document until you remove them or close the account.
