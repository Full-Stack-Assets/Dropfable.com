# Ranked buyer map

Do not lead with Etsy Corporate Development. Etsy is a channel. They sold Depop to focus on unique goods and agentic shopping, and they prohibit AI prompt bundles.

## Tier 1 — tuck-in into an existing file/seller audience

| Buyer | Fit | First ask | Path |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Creative Fabrica | Craft files + AI Spark | Generate disclosure-ready files for their catalog | Founder / partnerships, not IR |
| Inmagine Group (123RF, Pixlr, TheHungryJPEG, CraftBundles) | Historical marketplace tuck-ins | Maker-tool add-on like Pixlr | BD / corp-dev |
| Shutterstock / Envato | Placeit pattern: buy a simple maker | Plug generation into Elements | Partnerships |
| Design Bundles / Font Bundles | Same SKUs Dropfable generates | Co-list Sales Kits | Operator email |

## Tier 2 — Etsy-seller SaaS (first commercial conversations)

EverBee, eRank, Alura, Marmalead, SaleSamurai. They sell “find the niche.” Dropfable is “now make the product.” Start with a co-sell or “generate listing from this keyword” pilot.

## Tier 3 — large strategic (only after unique data or a Canva App)

- Canva: `strategic-partnerships@canva.com` and Canva Developers. Corp Dev / CLO: Todd Carpenter. They buy visual AI and design suites, not Gemini wrappers.
- Adobe Express / Firefly / Stock — slower, legal-heavy.
- Shopify — only with proven digital-download GMV.
- Gumroad — partner, weak acquire path.

## Tier 4 — financial backup

Acquire.com, Quiet Light, Empire Flippers, FE International — **after** verified Stripe TTM profit. Typical micro-SaaS: ~3–5x trailing profit.

## Non-targets for first outreach

Etsy IR, random PE without a CIM, generic “AI platform” buyers who can rebuild this in a sprint.
