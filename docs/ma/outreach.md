# Partnership-first outreach

Corp-dev does not respond to “please acquire my app.” Use this sequence.

1. Fill [teaser.md](./teaser.md) with **evidenced** numbers only.
2. Warm path: sellers who already use EverBee or Creative Fabrica; Canva Apps if listed; LinkedIn to Head of Partnerships / Seller Tools.
3. Send the teaser. Ask for a **90-day pilot**, not an LOI.
4. Instrument attach rate: kit downloaded → “I listed this” → sale note (already in the Archive UI).
5. If a buyer leans in: NDA, short CIM, [data room](../data-room/README.md).
6. If two or three pilots produce no strategic interest: list on Acquire.com with Stripe-connected financials.

## Email skeleton (Tier 1 / 2)

Subject: Pilot — generate listable digital products from a niche your sellers already research

We built Dropfable for sellers who know the niche and still have to write the product. One click returns a complete file plus Etsy/Gumroad copy and a Sales Kit ZIP, with Etsy AI disclosure baked in. Prompt packs are Gumroad-only.

Would you run a 90-day pilot: your keyword or catalog in, a disclosure-ready listing package out? We will share attach-rate, not vanity AI metrics.

Live: https://dropfable.com

## Canva

Do not email founders. Start at strategic-partnerships@canva.com and the Canva Apps developer program. Treat a Canva App as distribution, not the acquisition thesis.

## Brokers

Do not hire an investment bank pre-revenue. Use a broker only if trailing profit can absorb 10–15% and still clear a meaningful check.
