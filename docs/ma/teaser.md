# Dropfable — confidential teaser

**What it is:** A seller-side manufacturing tool. A creator enters a niche and downloads a complete digital product (planner, guide, checklist, template pack, or swipe file) plus Etsy/Gumroad listing copy, cover art, and a Sales Kit ZIP.

**What it is not:** A marketplace. Not an Etsy corp-dev target. Prompt packs are Gumroad-only (Etsy bans AI prompt bundles).

**Customer:** Digital-download sellers who already research niches in EverBee/eRank and still have to write the product.

**Why a buyer cares:** Attach generation to an existing seller or craft-file audience. One download closes the loop from keyword to listable file.

**Ask:** 90-day distribution partnership (generate SKUs or “make this listing” from a keyword). Acquisition conversation after attach-rate is measured.

**Do not include in outbound until evidenced:** revenue, user counts, listing-to-sale conversion.

Live: https://dropfable.com
